# HybridCipher Desktop Privacy Notice

Version: 2026-03-24

This notice describes the local and service-related data handling relevant to HybridCipher Desktop.
Your use of HybridCipher services may also be subject to separate privacy policies, organizational data-handling rules, or account agreements published by HybridCipher or your organization.

## 1. Local device data

HybridCipher Desktop may store data on your device including:

- local session state
- device identifiers and trust state
- protected folder metadata
- updater state
- logs and diagnostics generated during app use
- local preferences and acceptance records for app notices or legal prompts

This local data is used to operate the desktop client and recover normal workflows after restart.

## 2. Service communication

When connected to a HybridCipher service, the app may transmit data required for authentication, device onboarding, encrypted file operations, transparency verification, diagnostics, update checks, and other service features you choose to use.

## 3. Diagnostics and support

If you submit feedback or support material from the app, the content you choose to send may include your email address, message text, attachments, and diagnostic context needed to investigate an issue.

## 4. Security

HybridCipher Desktop is designed so encryption-sensitive operations happen on the client. Even so, no software or networked service can guarantee absolute security, and you remain responsible for protecting the device you use.

## 5. Retention and organizational policies

Your organization or service operator may apply separate policies for accounts, logs, backups, and retention. Those policies are outside this local desktop notice and may be governed by separate agreements.

## 6. Website and service policies

HybridCipher may publish broader website or service privacy policies that apply to account management, support, marketing pages, or hosted services. This desktop notice specifically describes the installed desktop client and its local-device behavior.
